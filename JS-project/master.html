Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our masterclass

How long does the masterclass take to complete?

The masterclass is designed to be completed in 8-12 weeks if you dedicate 5-7 hours per week. However, you have access to all materials for 12 months from your enrollment date, so you can learn at your own pace. Many students choose to revisit certain modules as they progress in their careers.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes! We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with the masterclass within the first 30 days of enrollment, simply contact our support team and we'll refund your payment in full, no questions asked.

What if I can't attend the live sessions?

All live sessions are recorded and available to watch on-demand within 24 hours. You'll also be able to submit questions that our instructors will answer in future sessions or via the community forum. Many students successfully complete the program using only the recorded materials.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible monthly payment plans for all our programs. You can split the total cost into 3, 6, or 12 monthly payments with no additional fees. Payment plans are available at checkout when you enroll.

Will this help me if I'm looking to change careers?

Absolutely! Approximately 40% of our students use the masterclass to transition into new industries. Our career coaches specialize in helping professionals make successful career changes and will work with you to develop a tailored transition strategy. The hands-on projects are particularly valuable for building experience in your new field.

How does the career coaching work?

Our career coaching includes personalized 1-on-1 sessions (frequency depends on your plan) where you'll work with an experienced career strategist. They'll help you with resume optimization, LinkedIn profile enhancement, interview preparation, salary negotiation strategies, and personalized job search tactics. Professional and Enterprise plan members also get direct feedback on their application materials.
